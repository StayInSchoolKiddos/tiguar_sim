The candidate matches the May 1 cruise/loiter operating region, but independent April 30 climb prediction misses the proposed 0.5 m/s target. Do not interpret successful setpoint tracking as a validated thrust curve or complete aerodynamic model.

Evidence and data separation

Two BINs yield 3 flights on April 30 and 13 on May 1. Selection retained 8 and 35 nonoverlapping ten-second, flaps-up windows, respectively. After rejecting advance ratios outside the supplied propeller table, 7 held-out and 31 training windows remain for the propulsion/energy comparison. The training windows span only 22.68–24.24 m/s EAS. These data cannot identify a broad airspeed/throttle map or a full drag polar.

Inputs: both supplied PDFs, Moments of Inertia.docx, both BINs, and the user's approximate 55 lb takeoff mass including landing gear. ArduPlane is pinned to bbf0b066e4baa04b5a746f22de642a25905cd52d; JSBSim is 1.3.3. Real throttle is RCO2.C16 with time-dependent servo parameters applied. Simulation outputs are explicitly remapped to equivalent axes 1–4.

Throttle, RPM, airspeed, and altitude response

Fit: throttle → equivalent shaft power → propeller RPM/thrust. Drag is constrained through the energy balance using logged TAS, climb and acceleration. Only effective thrust scale and zero-lift drag are fitted jointly; induced drag k=0.04 is fixed. Thrust is inferred, not measured. The fitted zero-lift drag coefficient is 0.00790, and effective thrust multiplier is 1.064.

Adjustments and unresolved assumptions

  • Span changed from 4.10 to 3.888 m; provisional trapezoidal area 1.041 m² and mean aerodynamic chord follow the reported root/tip chords. Approximate HQ airfoil labels were not treated as exact coordinate data.
  • Mass set to approximately 55 lb (24.95 kg), including gear. Actual fuel burn and CG are unknown. Loaded CG is provisionally x=0.94 m, z=0 on the model datum.
  • Loaded inertias are 20.5/15.5/34 kg·m². Yaw 34 is a prior: the supplied 41.4 cannot coexist with the supplied roll/pitch values for one configuration about a common origin. Fixture corrections and measurement configurations remain uncertain.
  • Effective roll/pitch/yaw moment-function scales are 4.415/3.780/7.411. They preserve approximate seed angular response with the larger inertias; they are not independently identified aerodynamic coefficients. Controller gains were kept fixed.
  • Ground geometry now has tricycle contacts and 0.713 m track; longitudinal positions, height and friction are provisional. Ground handling and landings are not validated.
  • Signed elevator drag was corrected to an even, nonnegative deflection term; the effective aileron combination no longer cancels opposed surfaces; propeller inertia has explicit units.
  • The engine is an empirical equivalent shaft-power source, not a detailed DA35/hybrid model. Electric boost usage, actual propeller performance and fuel consumption remain unresolved.
  • Equivalent control surfaces retain seed travel conventions. Physical ruddervator travel, flap effects, stall/post-stall response and product-of-inertia values need further work.

Failed experiments retained

The first candidate with the seed vertical CG offset pitched up and stalled. An initial baseline attempt encountered a stale JSBSim socket and is explicitly invalid; it is excluded from all comparisons here. The harness now checks the socket and tracks child processes for cleanup. A larger-inertia model without corresponding moment adjustments oscillated and failed altitude/airspeed checks. Its artifacts remain in runtime/candidate_final; the tested revision is runtime/candidate_final_scaled.
