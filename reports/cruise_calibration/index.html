These are fit-point consistency checks, not independent accuracy estimates: the power table was constructed from these same targets.

What the results show

  • The first supported fleet target is 10-15% throttle (4156 RPM). Excluded bins: 0-1, 1-5, 5-10%. Their advance ratios exceed the supplied propeller table. Simulated values there are diagnostics, not validated windmilling behavior.
  • Maximum shaft power changes from 2363 W to 1616 W. The highest measurement represents the 80–100% bin midpoint (90%), so 90–100% power is held constant provisionally.
  • The gray band is aircraft/configuration spread, not measurement uncertainty. Matching the average does not reproduce every aircraft.
  • The 21–25 m/s curves show aerodynamic load sensitivity at fixed throttle and density. They are predictions; the spreadsheet has no speed-resolved observations to validate them.
  • Low-throttle baseline shape is rescaled to meet the first supported target. Idle, windmilling, fuel use and throttle transients remain uncalibrated.
  • Thrust coefficients and airframe drag remain fixed. Better spreadsheet RPM agreement alone does not establish better thrust or climb accuracy.
